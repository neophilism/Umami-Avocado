# Security and scientific operating boundaries

- Research-only until independent food and regulatory review; no result establishes edibility.
- Never commit passwords, vendor credentials, samples identifying private persons, or confidential SOWs.
- All lab orders, payments, biological shipment and environment-release controls are disabled by default.
- Do not interpret fake instrument output as measured science. Do not design or execute high-risk biological workflows without appropriate review.
- Future authorization is checked by trusted code, not AI prose. Report security concerns through private owner contact rather than public issues.
